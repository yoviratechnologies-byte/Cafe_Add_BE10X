Our Philosophy

From High-Elevation Farms Directly into Your Cup

At Aura, we believe coffee is an architectural discipline. We collaborate with independent family-owned estates across Chikmagalur, Coorg, and Yirgacheffe, paying 45% above Fairtrade minimums to support regenerative agriculture.

Every batch is micro-roasted in small 5kg drums to highlight natural floral acidity, complex stone-fruit undertones, and toasted honeycomb sweetness.
